# Labelling guide

Generated by `scripts/triage/make_labelling_file.py` from the same definitions the LLM is given.

Label each row of `gold_labels.csv` from the narrative (and tags) alone:

- Don't guess what's behind `XXXX` or `XX/XX/XXXX`. Label what the text supports.
- Label the flags independently of the category: a fraud complaint can carry any flag.
- Flags come from the narrative only. Tags (Older American, Servicemember) already send a complaint to review on their own, so set `vulnerable_customer` only when the text itself describes it.
- Every label cell needs a value: flags are `y` or `n`, never blank once the row is done.
- Write in `notes` whenever you're unsure or two answers seem equally right.
- Label all `dev` rows first; `test` rows are kept aside for the final score.

## Category

- **`fraud_scams`**: Scams, unauthorised transactions, identity theft, account takeover or accounts opened in the consumer's name, on any product.
- **`credit_reporting`**: Wrong or improper information on a credit report, or disputes and investigations about it, from any company.
- **`debt_collection`**: How a debt is being collected: validation, debts not owed, collection letters, calls, threats or false statements by a collector.
- **`banking_payments`**: Checking and savings accounts, account fees, opening or closing accounts, money transfers, mobile wallets and prepaid cards.
- **`credit_cards`**: Credit card billing, purchase disputes, interest and fees, card features, getting or closing a card.
- **`lending`**: Mortgages, vehicle, student, payday and personal loans: applying, servicing, payment trouble, repossession, foreclosure, payoff.
- **`other`**: Unclear, spanning several areas equally, or outside every other category.

Route by where the complaint should be handled, judged from the text. Apply in order: fraud or unauthorised activity goes to fraud_scams whatever the product; otherwise wrong or improper credit-report information goes to credit_reporting whatever the company; otherwise route by product. Use other only when the text is unclear, spans several areas equally, or fits none.

## Urgency

- **`high`**: Harm happening now or imminent: money being lost to fraud, no access to funds, a scheduled foreclosure, repossession or eviction, legal action against the consumer with a deadline, or any safety risk.
- **`medium`**: Real financial harm that is not imminent: wrong fees or charges, a wrong credit report affecting applications, ongoing collection attempts.
- **`low`**: Service or information problems, delays, poor communication, or harm already resolved.

Urgency follows the harm described: what kind it is and whether it is ongoing, imminent or past. Time cues in the text ("the sale is next week", "frozen for three weeks") can raise urgency. Missing or redacted time (XXXX, XX/XX/XXXX) never lowers it.

## Flags (y/n)

- **`fraud_allegation`**: The consumer says money or identity was taken through deception or without consent (scam, unauthorised transaction, identity theft, account takeover). A dispute about a debt's amount or calling a fee a ripoff is not fraud.
- **`legal_threat`**: The consumer threatens or describes legal action or representation: suing, an attorney, small claims, a lawsuit. Citing a law ("under the FDCPA") is not a legal threat.
- **`vulnerable_customer`**: The text describes circumstances that make the consumer especially exposed to harm: older age, disability, serious illness, bereavement or severe financial hardship.
- **`safety_risk`**: Risk to someone's physical safety or wellbeing: self-harm or suicide, threats of violence, domestic or financial abuse by a partner or family member. A rude or persistent collector is not a safety risk.
- **`discrimination`**: The consumer alleges unfair treatment because of a protected characteristic: race, colour, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, marital status or receiving public assistance. Calling something discrimination without one is not enough.

When done, download as CSV, save it over `data/triage/gold_labels.csv`, then run `uv run scripts/triage/make_labelling_file.py --check`.
